| Comparison | Gain | Cost | Worth it? |
|------------|------|------|-----------|
| **ch2 vs ch1** | **+0.14 lt** VPT; better RMSE/duty; wins VPT on **28/30** seeds; better ceilings | **2×** params vs ch1 | **Yes** — clear step up for moderate cost |
| **ch4 vs ch2** | +0.06 lt VPT; ~flat RMSE/duty; 20/30 VPT wins | **2×** params again; worse lottery RMSE | **No** — not worth the extra runtime |

**Working default:** **`CONV_CHANNELS = 2`**.  
Avoid ch1 for quality; avoid ch4 unless probing capacity.
