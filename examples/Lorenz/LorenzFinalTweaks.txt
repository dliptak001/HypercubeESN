1. INPUT_SCALING grid (do this first)

You sit at 0.04. Free-run is usually more sensitive to this than to almost anything else.

┌──────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Try              │ Why                                                                         │
├──────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 0.02, 0.03       │ quieter drive → less error amplification in closed loop                     │
├──────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 0.05, 0.06, 0.08 │ stronger drive → better use of reservoir (if you aren't already saturating) │
└──────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Test: one seed (or 3), fixed DIM/M/epochs, change only INPUT_SCALING, OrbitSweep Unseen.
If one side wins by a clear margin on mean VPT×duty, lock it and re-run SeedSweep.




3. Spectral radius a notch off the edge

SR = 0.99 is near-chaos. Free-run sometimes prefers a slightly contractive reservoir.

┌──────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Try              │ Why                                                     │
├──────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 0.95, 0.97, 0.98 │ less amplification of prediction error on the attractor │
└──────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Cheap; often flat; still worth one matched survey.
